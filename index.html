📖 How to Play - Rivals Guide

1. Ground Spawning & Controls

Fighters spawn safely on solid elevated ground platforms! Move using WASD or Arrow Keys.

2. Fortnite Scoped Aiming & Active Abilities

Hold Right-Click to aim down sight with a Fortnite-style scope crosshair! Press [E] or tap the on-screen Ability button to trigger your outfit's active ability (e.g. Cheetah Dash, Ninja Cloak, Cyber Barrier)!

3. Weapon-Specific Mastery Skins (500 Damage)

Deal 500 Damage with a specific weapon to unlock an exclusive 👑 Gold Mastery Skin for that weapon only!

4. New Admin Commands

Press [~] to run admin commands: dodge (auto-dodge incoming bullets), aimbot (auto-target), and homing (tracking bullets)!
