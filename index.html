📖 How to Play - Rivals Guide

1. Fast Movement & Platform Jumping

Use WASD or Arrow Keys to sprint and jump across 3D platforms endlessly. On mobile, pull the left joystick for rapid movement.

2. Aiming & Combat Controls

Aim with your Mouse Cursor and Left-Click to shoot. Press [R] to reload ammo and [G] to lob explosive bombs at rival fighters (Sphinx Exclusive)!

3. Earn Victory Credits & 3 Leaderboards

Score kills (+50 Credits) and win matches (+50 Victory Bonus Credits) to climb 3 separate Global Leaderboards: Kills, Credits, and Damage!

4. Factions, Fragments & Blueprints

Pick Dark or Light allegiance to complete Phantom/Celestial Challenges! Collect 200 Weapon Fragments to unlock weapons or spin the Daily Wheel for secret blueprints.
